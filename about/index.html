About

Ph.D. in Physics. Applied scientist in machine learning.

I work on machine learning methods, production-scale systems, and applications in science.

I work on learning systems for reasoning, structured data, and scientific applications.

I am a Senior Applied Scientist in AWS AGI Foundations. My current work focuses on agentic reinforcement learning, LLM post-training, and foundation models for structured data, including time series and tables.

Before AWS, I was an AI Researcher at Shell, developing physics-informed and weakly supervised methods for scientific data. I earned my Ph.D. in Physics from Harvard University, where I worked with Xiaowei Zhuang on computational and super-resolution microscopy.

My background spans computational microscopy, physics-informed machine learning, and large-scale foundation models.
