Research

Models that learn, reason, and adapt.

My work connects foundation-model pretraining, reinforcement learning, multimodal reasoning, and scientific structure. The goal is useful intelligence that remains efficient, grounded, and robust.

Current direction / 01

Reasoning with specialized knowledge.

01 / Reasoning

Time-series knowledge injection

SenTSR-Bench studies how domain specialists can inject observations directly into a general model's reasoning trace, improving diagnostic reasoning without updating the general model's weights.

AISTATS 2026 paper
02 / Foundation models

Multisensor geospatial intelligence

msGFM unifies optical, radar, elevation, and multispectral observations through cross-sensor masked pretraining over two million images, then transfers to segmentation, classification, cloud removal, and pan-sharpening.

CVPR 2024 paper
03 / AI for science

Generative models with physical structure

PreDiff combines probabilistic latent diffusion with explicit knowledge alignment. The model can represent uncertain futures while steering its denoising process toward scientifically meaningful constraints.

NeurIPS 2023 paper
04 / Multimodality

Learning from multimodal context

CaMML introduces a lightweight hierarchical module for integrating long, domain-specific multimodal contexts into large models. The work received an ACL 2024 Area Chair Award.

ACL 2024 paper
05 / Efficient systems

Retrieval and generation in parallel

PipeRAG co-designs the algorithm and serving system to overlap retrieval with generation, adapt retrieval intervals, and balance search quality against latency. It reaches up to 2.6x lower end-to-end latency.

KDD 2025 paper

Research program / 02

Five connected questions.

01

Agentic learning

How can skills, policies, tools, and feedback co-evolve during reinforcement learning?

02

Structured data

How can broad synthetic priors produce capable tabular and time-series foundation models?

03

Multimodal context

How should models retrieve, compress, and reason over long contexts from multiple modalities?

04

Physical grounding

How can scientific constraints shape generative models without sacrificing uncertainty?

05

Efficient adaptation

How do we transfer, customize, and serve large models under practical resource limits?

06

Scientific discovery

How can machine learning expose structures that are difficult to observe or measure directly?
